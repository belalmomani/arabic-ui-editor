## English

Arabic UI Editor is an Android app for designing app screens and exporting code.

**Data we collect:** We collect no personal data on our own servers and operate none. There are no user accounts. The app shows Google AdMob ads (see below).

**Stored on your device only:** your projects, images, sounds and settings, in the app's private storage. Cloud backup is disabled.

**Ads (Google AdMob):** a banner on the projects screen and an interstitial after a successful export or build. AdMob automatically collects the advertising ID and other device IDs, IP address (approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Consent is requested through Google's UMP form where required. You can reset/delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalization in Android Settings > Google > Ads. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites .

**Optional features that contact third parties only when you enable them:**
- *Build via GitHub:* you sign in with your own GitHub account (device flow). The access token stays on your device and is used to create a private repository in your account, upload your project files, run GitHub Actions and download the result. GitHub's policies apply.
- *AI assistance:* if you enter your own API key for a provider you choose, the text you write (app description / project content) is sent to that provider to generate designs or text. The key is stored on your device only and is never included in projects or exports. The provider's policies apply.
- *Sync folder:* copies of your projects are written only to a folder you pick with the system file picker.
- *Share/export:* files go only to the app or place you choose.

**Permissions:** Internet, network state and advertising ID.

**Children:** Not directed to children under 13.

**Security & deletion:** credentials are kept in the app's private storage; you can remove them in the app. Delete projects in the app, clear app data, or uninstall to remove everything.
